01 / THE IDEA

A little distance.
A better perspective.

An overlook is a place to pause and see clearly. That is the feeling this mail client should bring to a busy day: a settled place to read, think, and respond.

Overlook belongs to Last Refuge’s world of quiet mountains, warm light, and considered tools. Its own identity opens that world onto a horizon: spacious, attentive, and reassuring.

Last Refuge gives us the place. Overlook gives us the view.
